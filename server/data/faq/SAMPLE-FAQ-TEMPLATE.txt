ADMISSION PROCESS
-----------------
Q: What is the admission process for BTech?
A: Students must qualify JEE Main / JEE Advanced. Counselling is done through JoSAA for IITs/NITs or state counselling boards. Applications open in June every year.

Q: What is the last date to apply?
A: Applications for 2025-26 close on July 15, 2025. Late applications are not accepted.

Q: What documents are required for admission?
A: Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, JEE scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card, and passport-size photographs.

FEES STRUCTURE
--------------
Q: What is the total fee for BTech?
A: The total BTech fee is approximately ₹6.5 Lakhs over 4 years (₹1.6L per year). SC/ST students pay ₹10,000 per year. Fee can be paid semester-wise.

Q: Are there any scholarships available?
A: Yes. Merit-cum-means scholarships are available for students with family income below ₹5 LPA. Central Sector Scheme and state scholarships also apply.

Q: What is the hostel fee?
A: Hostel fee is ₹25,000 per semester including mess charges. AC rooms are available at ₹35,000 per semester.

COURSES & DEPARTMENTS
---------------------
Q: Which BTech branches are available?
A: Computer Science Engineering, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Data Science, and AI & ML.

Q: Is there a lateral entry for BTech?
A: Yes, diploma holders can join directly in 2nd year (3rd semester) through state lateral entry exam (LEET).

Q: Does the college offer MTech and PhD programs?
A: Yes. MTech is offered in 12 specializations. PhD programs are available in all departments with GATE fellowship of ₹12,400/month.

PLACEMENTS
----------
Q: What is the average placement package?
A: The average package for 2024 batch was ₹14.2 LPA. Highest package was ₹56 LPA offered by Google.

Q: Which companies visit for campus placements?
A: Top recruiters include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Deloitte, Samsung, Qualcomm, and Goldman Sachs. Over 300 companies participated in 2024.

Q: What is the placement percentage?
A: 92% of eligible students (opted for placements) received job offers. 85% of total batch was placed in 2024.

HOSTEL & FACILITIES
-------------------
Q: Is hostel accommodation guaranteed for all students?
A: First-year students are guaranteed hostel accommodation. Senior students are allotted based on CGPA merit.

Q: What facilities are available on campus?
A: 24x7 Wi-Fi, library with 50,000+ books, sports complex, gym, medical center, canteen, ATM, and a central computing facility with 500 systems.

Q: Is ragging allowed?
A: Ragging is strictly prohibited under UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations 2009. Any incident should be reported to the Anti-Ragging Committee or helpline 1800-180-5522.

EXAMS & ACADEMICS
-----------------
Q: What is the grading system?
A: 10-point CGPA grading system. Minimum CGPA of 5.0 required to pass. Students with CGPA below 4.0 for 2 consecutive semesters face academic probation.

Q: How many backlogs are allowed?
A: Students can have a maximum of 4 active backlogs at any time. More than 4 backlogs result in detention from moving to the next year.

Q: When are semester exams held?
A: End-semester exams are held in November-December (Odd semester) and April-May (Even semester). Mid-semester exams are in September and February.
